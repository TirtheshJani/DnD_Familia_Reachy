# Geography of the Nephria Region

Nephria is one of roughly fourteen cities in the wider setting. The campaign stays in and around Nephria for now.

## Rivers and water

- The Cobalt River flows from the far north to the southern sea and separates Nephria from Xeria. The mansions of Siren's Reach overlook it.
- The Blackwater flows down out of the Kulchith and divides Nephria itself. The Estates lie across it from the city proper.
- The Graywater: the deep water beneath the Graywater Crossing, the great bridge between the Estates and Old Town. The bridge stands about 300 feet above it.
- The southern ocean is the River Styx (see 01-world-cosmology.md). Locals call it the amnesia ocean.

## Land

- The Kulchith: what people call a jungle is really a temperate rainforest, fed by a river. It has entropic, destructive properties. Small agricultural settlements inside it collapse within five years. At its heart is what the DM calls the big doom forest. The Jorel Estate patrols its border.
- The central valley around the two rivers is marshy.
- The southern coast is flat land rising into a large hill (not quite a mountain), with cliffside hills resembling California redwood country.
- The surrounding woods hold terrible creatures. The whole region carries a sense of doom.

## Neighbours

- Xeria: the capital of the province (possibly the whole world), across the Cobalt to the north. The Sovereign rules from there. A monorail line runs from Nephria through the Kulchith into Xeria and onward north; the tracks were laid by a god.
- The vampire capital lies in another direction (name not yet given).
- Nephria sits at the centre of these dangers: amnesia ocean, vampire capital, doom forest. "The party in the centre of the bad."

## Peoples

The original inhabitants are an isolated tribe of indigenous merfolk who live at the Blooming Grotto below the city. They keep to themselves, farm kelp and other ocean goods, and trade with the city without being integrated into it. They are not politically neutral.

## Maps

The DM works from a rough hand map called the Rust map and is preparing a finished large-format version on Bristol board.
