# House Rules and Homebrew

Reconstructed from the character creation session and the Graywater session. This table modifies standard 5e in several places, so the SRD is not authoritative here. Everything below should be confirmed with the DM; items marked (unverified) rest on a single unclear mention.

## Ruleset
The table uses a mix of 2024-era class features (Origin feats granting +1 to an ability score, Bardic Inspiration and prepared-spell bards) with homebrew skills and Grim Hollow material. Which core edition governs disputes has not been confirmed.

## Custom skill system
Standard skills are modified and extended. Skills are grouped under ability scores, with two new groupings.

- Strength: adds Brahmi (unverified name) and masonry and smithing.
- Dexterity: adds Initiative as a skill (so initiative can take proficiency).
- Constitution: adds Concentration as a skill.
- Sanity (a homebrew ability or skill group): Revelation (to learn more about something that is going on), Weird (to interact with strange phenomena), Grounding, and Entropic Paradox (also heard as Void Paradox). A Sanity check was used to understand a time-distorting entity; proficiency adds the bonus and does not grant advantage.
- Minor skills: Ceremony, Carpentry, and Void Paradox were named.

## Saving throws and terminology
The DM calls saving throws "safeguards" (for example, "make a Dexterity safeguard"). Saving throws still occur every six seconds while dying.

## Injuries
When a critical hit lands on a character, the player may choose to take double damage or to take an injury instead. Injuries accumulate and get worse. (Grim Hollow-style.)

## Armour and equipment
- Light helmet: +2 AC. Jack chains: +1 AC, disadvantage on Stealth. Under-armour chain: +2 AC, disadvantage on Stealth. A build reached AC 20 by stacking these.
- Full plate costs 4,500 gold. Lighter armour allows up to +3 Dexterity.
- Copper armour is cheaper but loses effectiveness when the wearer takes a critical hit; magic items can mitigate this.
- Firearms exist and are made by the Ildevar Estate. A "whole-slicer gun" allows point-blank shooting without disadvantage (unverified name). The Gunner feat is in use.

## Hit points and feats
Hit points are rolled per level with Constitution modifier and feat bonuses; the Tough feat grants +2 HP per level. Grim Hollow feats are available. Level 4 offers an Ability Score Improvement (+2 to one or +1 to two) or a feat.

## Environmental rules
- Styx water exposure: minus 1d4 to all checks and saves for a while after limited exposure; prolonged exposure leads toward becoming Hollow.
- Deep night freezes the environment but not creatures; horrors descend; silence and shelter are the norm.
- Umbrella hours strengthen illusion; witching hours strengthen enchantment.

## Achievements
The DM runs a system of one hundred achievements, awarded in small envelopes. Playing a warlock unlocks a group-wide achievement. Achievements are given out when new players join.

## Table conventions
- Custom character sheets are designed on Canva.
- Initiative is sometimes rolled in pairs, with each pair using its higher roll.
- Inspiration is used to reroll, and a "lucky tarot" deck exists in play (unverified mechanics).
- Guidance may be granted freely out of combat but costs an action in combat.
- Sanity checks can be boosted by Flash of Genius and inspiration like any other check.

## Homebrew mechanics seen in play
- Reliquary shards: a resource tracked by at least one character, which can be used to perceive things (unverified).
- Mana wands: consumable spell wands (Create Water was cast from one: up to 10 gallons over a 30-foot radius).
- A large rock with googly eyes serves as a receptacle of memory; freeing a memory from it means experiencing it yourself.
