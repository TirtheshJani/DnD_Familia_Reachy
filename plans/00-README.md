# Shadows over Nephria: Campaign Context

A curated, fiction-only knowledge set for the Shadows over Nephria campaign, assembled on 2026-09-11 from the DM's worldbuilding sessions (17 July 2026), the player registry survey, and the Graywater Crossing session (10 September 2026).

## What this is for

Two uses. First, as project knowledge for planning and writing around the campaign. Second, as the `notes` corpus for the Reachy familiar's memory: every file here can be dropped into `data/notes/` and indexed as-is.

## What was deliberately left out

The source recordings were made at a table with real people. Everything about those people (health, habits, jobs, scheduling, snacks, jokes at each other's expense) has been removed. Player names do not appear anywhere in these files. Character names do. If you add to this folder, keep that rule: fiction and rules only, never the people.

## Confidence markers

The sources are AI transcripts of noisy rooms. Where a name or fact is uncertain it is marked (unverified) or given with alternatives. Confirm at Session Zero before relying on it.

- 06-roster-and-npcs.md: the current party, companions, NPCs, and named places

- 08-tone-and-boundaries.md: inspirations and the table's content lines

- 10-nesh-canon.md: the DM's written setting canon (Nesh, the Song, memory, Hollows, the Titans, arriving at Nephria, the Twelve Declarations and Twenty-Five Doctrines). Overrides files 01 to 09 where they differ.

## Canon corrections from file 10

The world is named Nesh, the Waking Domain, a demiplane in the Astral Sea. The gate settlement is Northreach Crossing, not North Reach Plaza. Hollows fight back with reflective, radiant, and piercing power when provoked. The Sovereign's laws are the Twelve Declarations, interpreted by the Gold Masks through the Twenty-Five Doctrines. The coinage is the Crown, held by the Iron Bank of Maliburn. Two Titans of Memory, Eris and Thatos, dwell in the ocean.
