# Tone, Inspirations, and Table Boundaries

From the DM's player registry for Shadows over Nephria.

## The setting's inspirations
The wider world: Elden Ring, Bloodborne, The Witcher 3, The Elder Scrolls, The Wheel of Time, Ori and the Blind Forest, Journey, Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag, Over the Garden Wall, the SCP Foundation, Greek and Norse mythology, Grimm's Fairy Tales.

Nephria specifically: BioShock Infinite, Candela Obscura, the Magic: The Gathering colour pie, MTG: New Capenna, Eberron, Hades II, Dredge, Subnautica, Bridgerton, Downton Abbey, The Fall of the House of Usher, Arcane, with 1920s and 1940s Art Deco, noir, and organised crime aesthetics.

## The DM's description
A home setting refined over a decade, part of a much larger world of about fourteen cities, still shaped by play. A water cycle with amnestic properties, nightly temporal disturbances, unseen horrors in the deepest hours, a cycle of reincarnation, and metallurgic decomposition.

## Consent structure
The table uses three tiers: On-Board (welcome), Veiled (present but implied or off-screen), Off-Limits (never present, never joked about). A request to stop a topic is not open for debate.

Currently On-Board for the group (any player may veil or remove): blood, bug horror, claustrophobia, demons, freezing to death, natural disasters, paralysis and physical restraint, thirst, severe weather.

Currently Veiled for the group (any player may remove): cancer, eyeball horror, gaslighting, genocide, gore, police aggression, rat horror, romance between PCs, romance between PCs and NPCs, starvation, terrorism, torture.

Off-Limits for this game: explicit descriptions of sex; explicit harm or cruelty to children and animals; heatstroke; homophobia; overt references to real-world religion; pregnancy horror, miscarriage, and abortion; racism; self harm; sexism; sexual assault; spiders; and the character names Amber, Bella, Dana, and Mike.

## Implications for a familiar at this table
Anything the familiar says or generates must respect the Off-Limits list absolutely and treat the Veiled list as things to allude to, never describe. It never invents a character with a banned name. It never speaks for the DM. During deep night, in fiction, everyone falls silent; a familiar that goes still and quiet when the DM declares deep night is playing the world correctly.
