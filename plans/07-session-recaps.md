# Session Recaps (fiction only)

## Before the Graywater session
Events referenced in play, in rough order:

- Nixon's time adventure "with the plums," including a woman in a forest cottage pretending to be a hag who moved travellers through time by way of arches. The party killed her. Dark Nixon appeared during this period and later dissolved in sunlight.
- A visit to a noble Estate about a month before the Graywater session, for a coming-of-age ceremony where a child's bloodline magic awakened in a flare of radiant light.
- A captain was seen gilding at supernatural speed, before the party's eyes.
- The party found strange body parts, now kept in a bag of holding, and Jam wanted answers about them and about the shadow entity. No one had come back to him with a timeline.
- The party explored the Jade Catacombs.
- In the jailhouse, the prisoner Waja was murdered. Osteomancy was used on the body. The party gathered names and locations of those involved.
- An interrogation of the two people running a pub on the Crossing revealed a manifest for the bombs the party is hunting.
- Two of the party went below to retrieve the manifest and found a door rigged to explode. The party detonated it. Behind it was a portal to a well of blackness. One of them stepped through and met the entity with eyes for a voice, who said "You are not one of my warlocks," let him leave, then began to hunt him. The entity came out of the gate after them. The party fled the nightclub and ran across the scrapyard until their lungs burned, then rested at the base of some stairs.

## The Graywater Crossing session (10 September 2026)

### The setup
Late night on the Graywater Crossing, an hour from deep night, crowds thinning. Jam, determined to get answers, walked the bridge and heard a familiar melody: Nixon finishing a set on the patio of The Greyest Water. Nixon joined him. Thraka, who had received Jam's text ("Shadow daddy"), approached from the other direction and stopped in front of the clinic beside the constabulary, near The Drowned Pearl.

### The explosion
The constabulary roof blew out in a fireball that expanded off the side of the bridge. Roofing tiles and porcelain rained down; Thraka took shrapnel. The watch captain pushed the door open and ushered out people with severe burns. Thraka fought the fire with Ray of Frost on the roof beams; Nixon confessed he makes fires rather than putting them out. Jam cast Create Water from a mana wand and rained on the whole front of the building. Small flame-creatures, later called rafflings, kept squeezing out through the cracks around a bolted metal door and sputtering out before they reached the open air. From inside the clinic came a swell of radiant light, exactly like the coming-of-age flare a month earlier: someone had come into their power.

### The rest of the party arrives
Alar, Stormak, and Clover arrived from the Jade Catacombs and the casino with a prisoner in tow. They had seen three explosions at this spot, one an hour, over three hours. Jam, Nixon, and Thraka had seen one and been there ten minutes. Insight revealed the explosions were identical: the same blast, the same clatter, the same splash of debris in the water. The bridge beyond was empty; a man Nixon had watched leave the shop a minute earlier should still have been visible and was not.

### The bubble
Anyone who tried to run more than about thirty feet from the site found themselves back where they started. Random bystanders were stuck too. The party worked out the ratio: about one minute inside for every eighteen to twenty outside, with the explosion repeating on a roughly fifteen-minute cycle from within. Jam's theory, from the plum adventure, that timeshifted places are entered under an arch went nowhere; no one had passed under one.

### The clinic
A scream of bloody murder came from the clinic. The party rolled initiative in pairs and went in. The interior was pitch dark to most eyes: beds, a central operating table, and standing over it a shadowed figure with two lightning-wreathed blades that proved to be spinning chain-scythes. Psychic damage did nothing. Radiant damage did double. Nixon's starry wisp outlined it in shivering light so it could not turn invisible; the clerics poured in guiding bolts; Thraka's draining strikes hit for forty; Jam's holy water bombs for sixty-two. Its scythe swept everyone within fifteen feet for necrotic and lightning damage, and it took legendary actions to swipe again. A level-two raven spell burned its legendary resistance. Rafflings manifested from coals and swarmed Thraka. Jam heated the operating table under its feet with magical tinkering.

### The betrayal mechanic
The entity fed on the party hurting each other. It forced Nixon to fire on an ally with a wisdom-save failure, and when Clover's guiding bolt dispelled its form "for the Sovereign," the shadow rushed across the floor and possessed Jam. Possessed, Jam hurled the burning metal table into the doorway, catching Clover and Stormak, and shot Clover in the face with the pulse launcher. Mr Boom Boom's force ballista knocked Jam into a corner. Each time allies hurt each other, the entity regained its legendary resistance. Clover and Stormak went down; Alar's healing words brought them back.

### The resolution
Alar cast a fifth-level dispelling on Jam. Nixon, rolling a Sanity check with Entropic Paradox, Flash of Genius, and inspiration (a total of 38), understood the creature: not a monster but emotional residue, a being that had decided to stay and relive its memories, trapping the party in that cycle with it. Nixon, who had lived so long among the dark things of the woods that he was part of that darkness, was given a choice: banish it to the woods, or take it inside himself. He took it in. The rafflings guttered out. The shadows dimmed into morning light.

### Aftermath
The night was lost to the loop, but everyone survived. The bomb manifest and Waja's killers remain open threads. Dark Nixon is still unaccounted for. The entity behind the portal is still out there. Jam's gift to Alar, a tentacle rod, was accepted. Clover and Stormak have a standing arrangement to heal each other.
