# Factions and Powers

## The Sovereign
Ruler of the province, seated in Xeria. Cares about everyone in her realm, and issues sweeping prohibitions (no statues of anyone but her, forbidden kinds of music, laws about amnesia). "In the name of the Sovereign" is invoked to justify a great deal. Described at the table as terrifying.

## The Priory
White-aligned hilltop institution that interprets the Sovereign's law for Nephria, keeps the archives, and houses the administration. Bans unapproved statues and certain music within the city.

## The yarl's coven (the Obsidian Quarter)
A Norse-styled coven headed by a noble yarl. Runs civic infrastructure, parks and planning, the Black Glass Terminal by association, and the fighting pit in the Gloam Walk.

## The Emerald Lady
Co-founder of Nephria. Lives in the great tree at the heart of the Emerald Quarter. Little else is known.

## The noble houses of the Heights
Rival houses compete for control of the Silk Quarter's casinos, fashion, and nightlife. Named houses have not yet appeared in play.

## The Estates
Across the Blackwater stand the Estates: Victorian-styled houses that are covens of witches rather than nobility. All practise some agriculture or animal husbandry and supply the city. Where the Heights answers deep night with a party, the Estates answer with spellcraft: glyphs and hexes that protect them from what is above.

### The council Estates (major influence in the city)
- Cassius: the noble face of the Estates. Parlays with the rest of the city and speaks on the Estates' behalf.
- Rune Shield: traditionalists, specialists in rune magic, and the ones who effectively run the Estates.
- Trinivane (also heard as Trinifane, Trinity): the devoted. The people you go to when you need to know something. Background options for characters from this Estate include lesser noble, firstborn of an attendant's house, or a nobody who takes the surname Trinifane (unverified spelling).
- Ildevar: fire and metal witches. Construct makers and rune crafters who turn scrap into constructs enchanted with gemstone. They make guns. Informally control the Scrap Ward.

### The specialised Estates (further-flung interests)
- Jordana: idealists in harmony with nature; the agricultural specialists.
- Jorel: mages through and through; they also patrol the border of the Kulchith to keep things from coming in.
- Durnagall: built on graves that predate the city. Burial services, and the ongoing work of re-sealing old graves so that nothing gilded climbs out. Associated with a Death and Burial Coven that works to prevent gilding.

## The merfolk of the Blooming Grotto
Indigenous, isolated, self-sufficient, trading kelp and ocean goods. Not neutral, not integrated.

## Gods and belief
The song at the world's heart (see 01-world-cosmology.md). Characters in Nephria may worship Styxian gods such as Eris. Warlock patrons seen or discussed at the table include a Great Old One of Cthulhu flavour and an undead or vampiric patron.

## The law on the Crossing
Two constabularies police the Graywater Crossing. Watch captains command guards; the guards fight fires with rope and bucket. At least one party member, Clover, is himself a constable.
