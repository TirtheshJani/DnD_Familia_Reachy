# Roster, Companions, NPCs, and Places

Names are as heard in a noisy recording. Alternatives are given where the transcript wavered.

## The Graywater party (the Wednesday game, 10 September 2026)
The familiar's owner does not yet have a character in this party; these are the characters present.

- Thraka (also heard Thraca, Brocka): an orc about seven feet tall with ash-grey skin, snow-white hair and beard, and face tattoos, wearing a heavy black burlap overcoat over very proper clothes, ready to shed the cloak and sit at a casino table. Warlock with a form of dread that reveals jagged mirrored limbs, draining strikes that grant temporary hit points, and a swarm companion. Tracks reliquary shards and has used osteomancy. Has a limited number of texts on a new cell phone, an ongoing joke.
- Stormak (also heard Tormek, Tarmac): an eight-foot goliath in heavy armour with a jotun war hammer and a bandolier of crossbows, tattooed face, white skin; described as a jive artist. Radiant caster (guiding bolt). Alar rides on his shoulder.
- Alar (also heard Malar, Molar, Pilar): a two-and-a-half-foot, three-eyed parrot with bright red feathers who would rather be at a party. Cleric-style radiant caster and healer (guiding bolt, healing word, aid). Knows Nixon from nighttime activities.
- Nixon: a blue-grey furbolg about seven feet tall, a former hermit who reinvented himself as bubbly. Musician who whistles constantly and plays sets at dive bars; "a little creepy on the side." Star-themed magic (starry wisp, an ecstatic surge, a wheel of fire). Long ago lived in dark woods among things that should have killed him. Had a whole time-travel adventure "with the plums." Has a shadow double, Dark Nixon, who dissolved in sunlight four days before the Graywater session and has not been seen since.
- Clover: a five-foot-six, ordinary-looking man with a boss cut (or bald), shabby clothes, small scars and bites all over his fingers, a rusty mace at his hip, and a clover necklace, his namesake. Gives off a lucky vibe. A constable, called "the cop" by the others. Cleric (spiritual weapon, guiding bolt, spell shield) who fights "for the Sovereign."
- Jam, full name Gem Cumbling (also heard Jem, Gem, Jim): a small gnomish boy with grey skin, a shock of flaming ginger hair, one green eye and one blue "eye" that is a stone, and a belt hung with contraptions and bombs. Artificer: Magnesis, Flash of Genius, magical tinkering, holy water bombs, a pulse launcher, vortex warp, an unseen servant. Was briefly possessed by the shadow entity.
- Mr Boom Boom: Jam's cannon companion, which wears Converse. Fires force ballista shots and has the Gunner feat. Sometimes the only thing visible when Jam is hidden.
- Rock: a big rock with googly eyes, a receptacle of memory, travelling with the party (unverified whether a character or an item).

## Recurring NPCs
- The watch captain of the Old Town constabulary on the Graywater Crossing: tall, muscular, breastplate, Nordic pulled-back hair. Ushered burn victims out of her burning station.
- The Sovereign (see 04-factions-and-powers.md).
- The Emerald Lady, co-founder of the city.
- Waja: a prisoner murdered in the jailhouse; the party has names and locations for those involved.
- The entity behind the portal: a thing that emerged from a well of blackness with eyes for a voice, saying "You are not one of my warlocks." It let an intruder leave, then hunted him.
- Dark Nixon: Nixon's shadow double, dissolved in sunlight.
- The hag who was not a hag: a woman in a forest cottage who pretended to be a hag and moved travellers through time. The party killed her.
- "Shadow daddy": the party's name for the shadow entity from the clinic basement (see 07-session-recaps.md).
- A pub manager and assistant on the Crossing who were interrogated about the bomb manifest.
