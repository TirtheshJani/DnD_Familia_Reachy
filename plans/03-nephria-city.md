# The City of Nephria

Aesthetic: BioShock Infinite meets New Capenna; 1920s and 1940s Art Deco, noir, organised crime, black glass, monorails, fireworks against the night.

## History

Founded about 120 years ago on a much older rune. Before that it was a mining and ranching town whose entire population vanished for reasons no one knows. Old Town is built on the ruins of Old Nephria. The Emerald Lady is one of the city's co-founders and still lives in the great tree of the Emerald Quarter.

## Getting in

The city is walled. Entry is through the North Reach Plaza, where a bureaucratic filter decides who gets a work visa. Visas require established family in the region. The system is transactional: you are useful to the city until you are not.

## Districts and their colours

The DM built the city on the Magic: The Gathering colour pie. Each district carries a colour and its meaning.

### North Reach Plaza (blue)
The entry slum. Blue for curiosity, ambition, hope for a better life, the will to improve oneself. People wait here for papers.

### The Obsidian Quarter, also called the Meridian (Norse coven)
A walled circular centre of tall plaza buildings: banks, offices, apartments, shops, gardens. Most of the city works here. Civic infrastructure is run by a Norse-styled coven led by a noble yarl, whose remit is parks, planning, and making the city beautiful.

- The Black Glass Terminal: hub of the Obsidian Rail, the black monorail network that runs through the city, out through the Kulchith, and on to Xeria.
- The Gloam Walk: the underground network beneath the Obsidian Quarter (think Toronto's PATH). The yarl's coven runs a fighting pit here. Parts of the Gloam Walk connect to the Jade Halls.

### The Heights (green)
The spired upper city with the great tree. Green for community and growth in any direction, the party spirit of "we are in this together," even when not for everyone's benefit. Three parts:

- The Silk Quarter: casinos, the fashion district, skyscrapers, server rooms, opulence, designer drugs, noble houses vying for control. Every night is a party. Deep night here is fireworks and artillery, not hiding.
- The Emerald Quarter: residential quarter of the nobility and socialites, dominated by the central tree where the Emerald Lady lives.
- Siren's Reach: the merchants' capital. Long quays where traders land; dignitaries and nobles visit; enormous mansions built into the cliffs overlooking the Cobalt.

### The Priory (white)
On the hilltop. White for order, control, doctrine. The Priory interprets the Sovereign's laws and finds the loopholes that let Nephria apply them without getting into trouble. The archives and administration buildings are here. The Lofts, where administration workers live, are built into the hillside overlooking the city.

### Old Town (red)
Built on Old Nephria. Red for passion, energy, creation. Old wood, old dance halls, cowboy and ranch-country architecture, iterated on with black glass apartments. This is where labour happens.

- The Scrap Ward and the Foundry: a huge scrapyard and foundry where things are made. Informally controlled by the Ildevar Estate.
- Rosewood Market: working-class market and labour sector, black glass apartments.
- Old Town is less protected during deep night than the Estates, but has fireworks to shoot things out of the sky.

### The Blooming Grotto
Home of the indigenous merfolk, below the city. See 02-geography.md.

### The Estates (across the Blackwater)
Victorian-styled noble houses that are in fact witch covens. See 04-factions-and-powers.md.

## Beneath the city: the Jade Halls

Under nearly everything lies the Jade Halls, older than the modern city: endless tunnels of nonsensically placed rooms, all solid jade. Staircases that lead nowhere, chasms that extend upward as well as down, geometry that could not physically exist. Haunting things live there. Networks within the Jade connect the Silk Quarter down to the docks, and the Gloam Walk passes through parts of it. The party has already been through the Jade Catacombs.

## Defence: the Obsidian Rail and the ADA

The Obsidian Rail is magic-tech. The same craft produced sentient illusions, adapted into the Automated Defense Artillery (ADA): illusions that fly during deep night and shoot down what comes from the sky. It is defence and it is spectacle; residents celebrate during the displays.

## The Graywater Crossing

A great bridge, about 120 feet across, joining the Estates (protected by witchcraft and hexes) to Old Town. Small stalls, shops, and buildings are built into the bridge along both edges, so you cannot see over the sides except down narrow alleys that open onto the deep water some 300 feet below. Late at night, casino and bar goers hurry home across it before deep night.

Known places on the Crossing:
- The Greyest Water: a dive bar built into the bridge, with a summertime patio out front. Nixon has played sets here.
- The Drowned Pearl: a general store.
- The constabulary: one of two on the bridge; this one oversees its side, Old Town, and the Old Town docks. A stable (empty of horses) and two side buildings adjoin it. Its watch captain is a tall, muscular woman in a breastplate with Nordic pulled-back hair.
- The clinic: a rudimentary two-storey clinic adjacent to the constabulary, with a front desk, beds, a central operating table, a barred wall, and a basement behind a heavy metal door. The site of the time-loop encounter (see 07-session-recaps.md).
- A nightclub and casino at the Old Town end, above which sits a portal to a black well (see 07-session-recaps.md).
- Two constabularies patrol the bridge; guards draw water from the Graywater by 300-foot rope and bucket when there is a fire.
