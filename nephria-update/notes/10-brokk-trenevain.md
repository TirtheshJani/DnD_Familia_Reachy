# Brokk Trenevain

Brokk Trenevain is Rocky's master. He is a Gloamhaunt, a sage by background, and he draws power two ways: as a bard, and as the warlock of a vampire. He is not a vampire himself, though he lets people wonder.

Confidence: the character sheet and the DM's cards are canon. Details from the 2 October session are reliable in outline; anything heard once in noisy audio is marked (unverified).

## Who he is

- Family: the Trenevain estate, a noble house across the Graywater Crossing. Walking from Old Town to the estate takes over an hour. His mother is alive.
- Age and look: about thirty, though he dresses younger than that and the look no longer quite fits. Average height (he is five foot seven and prefers to say six). Pale skin. He wears a cloak worn like a hood. Under the hood, his head is scarred.
- Manner: curious and watchful. He prefers to vanish and observe rather than act. He is warm and bubbly most of the time, then flares into anger that does not feel like his own, then calms just as fast.
- Alias: when strangers ask, he gives his name as Vlad.
- Languages: Neshian, Halfling, Jotun, and Nodal. Nodal is the language Rocky speaks.
- Talents: harmonica, oration, poetry, and performance. He reads the stars and the weather. He has an uncanny gift for psychometry, reading the history of things he touches.

## What he does not remember

Brokk has gaps. In Old Town a man named Kari greeted him as an old friend from childhood and spoke of his mother and of Trish, who was once his mother's lady's maid. Brokk recognised neither name. Kari's fear and hurt read as honest. This fits a world that takes memories.

## The Jade Halls

Brokk knows the Jade Halls beneath the city without ever having been there. He led Kari through them by instinct: rooms with no purpose, half-walls that must be climbed, two pools crossed on a path a foot wide, fourteen flights of stairs climbing higher than the city's surface, then a much longer stair down. None of it troubled him. It felt like home.

When he found a wall of the Halls broken open with a pickaxe, he was furious, and he turned that fury on Kari. When the tunnel began to close on them, he prayed in a trance, in a language he does not consciously know, to the deity of the Jade Halls. He calls that deity his own.

Eberhard, a gilded priest, also knows the Halls. The two of them share an unspoken kinship from that knowledge. Neither can explain it.

## Possessions of note

- Eyes of Charming: crystal-lensed glasses. He wears them. They let him cast Charm Person.
- Obsidian Mirror: a scrying mirror. (Carried; confirm with the DM.)
- Ancient Vacoran Amulet: tomb jade and small bones on a tangled chain. Kari took it from an iron chest in the newly opened chamber and was wearing it when the tunnel closed. Brokk later found it on the floor of the Grand Hall and pocketed it in secret. In the fiction he does not yet know what it does. Eberhard saw him bend to pick something up but did not see what it was.
- The Inkdrinker card: given by the DM at the end of the 2 October session. Its meaning is not yet known.
