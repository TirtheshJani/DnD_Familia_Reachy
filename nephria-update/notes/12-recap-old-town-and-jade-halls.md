# Recap: Old Town and the Jade Halls (2 October session)

Six days after the business at the Cassius estate. A Thursday in late autumn, the evening of a clear day with a cold, salty wind off the sea and a front of cloud on the horizon.

## Omens

Before the evening began, three cards were drawn from a corrupted tarot. Every card in that deck reads reversed. The World: inevitability and a disaster that has not quite fallen, a snake letting go of its tail, a new cycle after great loss. The High Priestess: deception, intuition blocked, a gap between the path taken and what one knows to be wise. The Sun: hope blocked, energy drained, light as cold and false as the gold packed round a buried monarch. Who drew which card was not clear.

## Three walks through Old Town

Krag spent the day at the fighting pits, collected his newly enchanted weapons, and went to soak at the bathhouse.

Brokk was walking alone, far from the Trenevain estate, when Kari bounded across the street to greet him as an old friend. Brokk did not know him. Kari tried again, and again, until Brokk simply fell into step beside him. Kari talked about Brokk's mother and about Trish, and about a chamber newly uncovered beneath the old dance hall, a ruin from an older city that Old Town was built over and one of the ways down into the Jade Halls. Kari hoped there might be loot to sell.

Eberhard was preaching on a corner outside his makeshift chapel, shaming a woman for her cloak under the Declaration of Beauty, fire licking round his fingers. Brother Caldus led him inside for potato and leek soup and told him to count to ten when angry. Eberhard pointed out that he had not burnt anyone.

## Down into the Halls

Though Kari brought him, Brokk led. He knew the way without knowing how. The Halls were endless jade: rooms with no purpose, waist-high walls to climb, a narrow path between two pools, fourteen flights up past where the surface should be and a longer stair down. Kari grew more afraid the deeper they went.

They found the new chamber. Someone had taken a pickaxe to the jade wall and left the pickaxe lying on the floor. Brokk was flooded with anger, accused Kari, and frightened him badly. Then the anger left him all at once. He lowered his hood and showed his scarred head.

## Rage in the square

Above them, Krag stepped out of the bathhouse refreshed, and a rage that was not his own rolled over him and over the whole square. Eberhard, who had been preaching meekly, turned to fury and burnt Corys, an orc carrying groceries and a doll, citing the Second Declaration. Corys fell, dying.

Krag aimed his rage at the man throwing fire and knocked Eberhard to the stones twice with his battleaxe. A dock worker who fought like a monk beat Eberhard down and turned on Krag as well. A grandmother slashed at Krag with a bladed fan. Brother Caldus healed Corys and calmed the crowd with a word. Only the grandmother kept raging.

## The chest

Beneath the dance hall, Brokk tried to charm Kari as a friend. Kari resisted and ran past him to a small round chamber with a raised dais and an old iron chest crusted over with jade. Brokk faded from sight and watched. Kari threw aside old books, tore open a small bag, and drew out a jade amulet hung with tiny bones. Brokk knew the pendant on sight but not what it did.

When Brokk reappeared, he played at recognising Kari. Kari half believed it. Then the chamber began to shrink. The five-foot passage they had come down was closing.

## The sinking

In the square the ground dropped a foot and rolled. Those who had bled watched their blood soak into the stones. Eberhard remembered the old rites: every soul goes to the land after death, every body returns to the earth, and every spilt drop of blood returns to the Sovereign. Beneath that tithe he sensed something older taking one of its own.

Krag's shadow rose and took him. He swung at the dock worker and missed. His vision blurred and he saw his three dead companions in the crowd. Eberhard rose to his knees and proclaimed that all who dwell beneath the Sovereign's peace owe duty in return, then cut his own palm with a paring knife and let the blood run into the ground. Brother Caldus slapped him down.

Everyone who had been hurt began to sink into the square as if into porridge of broken stone. Corys went under first. Krag fought it and could not pull free; at the end he told his dead friends he would be joining them. Eberhard sank looking up at the sky in blissful acceptance.

## The tunnel

Brokk dragged Kari by the collar towards the way out. Kari was putting on the amulet as they ran. The passage narrowed to a foot. At the bend Brokk let go of Kari to save himself, fell into a trance, and prayed to the deity of the Jade Halls in a tongue he did not know. His knees buckled. The walls closed. He felt his bones break, and then darkness.

## The Grand Hall

They woke, as if from a dream, on wet worked jade in total darkness, lying in a centimetre of water. Every body was whole and every wound healed. Eberhard lit the gilding on his own skin. There was no wall and no ceiling in reach of the light, only green floor.

The survivors: Krag, Brokk, Eberhard, Corys, and a young gnome called Till. Kari was not there. Brokk gave his name as Vlad. Brokk and Eberhard both knew this place for part of the Jade Halls, and a name came to Brokk unbidden: the Grand Hall. Both felt an unconscious kinship, as if the other were new to something they shared.

Brokk spotted Kari's amulet glinting thirty feet away. He threw an illusion of Kari's voice calling from the dark, and while everyone turned to it he picked the amulet up. Eberhard saw him bend down but not what he took.

From what they agreed to call north came the sound of waves lapping against a shallow edge: deep water nearby, and something moving in it, coming closer. Brokk sent four lights drifting out a hundred and twenty feet. Krag drew his greatsword and gathered the frightened ones behind him.

The session ended as Krag glanced over his shoulder and saw, in the darkness to his left, a massive serpentine shape.

## Threads left open

- Where Kari is, and whether the amulet saved anyone.
- What rage swept the square, and what older thing beneath the city takes a tithe of blood.
- Who pulled the sunken down into the Grand Hall and healed them.
- The serpent in the dark water.
- Whether Krag's hallucinations ended when he woke healed.
