# Roster additions, 2 October session

People Brokk and Rocky met or travelled with on 2 October. Player characters first, then everyone else. Spellings heard several ways in the recordings are listed so recall can match them.

## Player characters

### Brokk Trenevain
See `10-brokk-trenevain.md`. Also heard as Brock, Trinivane, Trinovane. Goes by Vlad with strangers.

### Krag
A half-orc fighter, about fifty-five. White hair, grey-green skin, six foot six and broad, heavier lately now that he has coin and eats well. Wears cold iron half plate (he wore old padded armour on earlier adventures). Uses a halberd as a walking stick, carries a greatsword across his back and a battleaxe at his hip. Speaks Orcish. Has no telephone and no wish for one.

Krag retired from adventuring and trains fighters at the fighting pits. A friend's funeral pulled him back in. He has had his weapons enchanted at a small smithy in Old Town. He carries a living shadow that can seize him when fortune turns against him.

His old adventuring party is dead: Alora Greenleaf, a tall blonde elf ranger in green; Benthar, a human wizard; and Grace Thunderfoot, a short, sturdy halfling rogue with a red ponytail. Since 2 October he has seen their faces in crowds.

Surname heard as Brittlebone and possibly Riddleball. (unverified) Also heard as Craig, Crag.

### Eberhard
A gilded priest of the Sovereign. Average height, a dancer's build, sunken sallow features, dirty-blond hair in a loose ponytail with shaved sides. Patches of his skin have turned to gold, enough to show he has been dead for some time. Wears a blood-red robe under vestments meant to look like gold that have gone a tarnished yellow-brown.

He preaches the Twelve Declarations on an Old Town street corner outside a makeshift chapel, a house converted with little money and a new stained-glass panel above its corner door. He swings between meek, halting preaching and furious zeal, and flame gathers round his hands when he points at sinners. Brother Caldus keeps him in check. Gilding grants him a reserve of vigour at the start of any fight.

Also heard as Ever heard.

## Others

- Kari. Brokk's childhood friend, though Brokk cannot remember him. Short, with halfling blood somewhere in his line. Loud striped clothes in purple and green. A performer, perhaps a dancer. He found the Vacoran amulet in a jade-crusted iron chest beneath the old dance hall and was wearing it when the tunnel closed. He was not among those who woke in the Grand Hall. Fate unknown. Also heard as Carry, Curry.
- Trish. Once lady's maid to Brokk's mother. A friend of Kari's. Her brother's friend was the explorer who opened the new chamber. Never seen.
- Brother Caldus. Has spent recent years on charitable and devotional work across Nephria's upper districts and is quietly praised for his sincerity. Visits Eberhard's chapel, cooks large pots of soup for whoever is hungry, sketches rooftops while he waits. Strong enough to catch a fist mid-swing. Healed the burnt Corys and calmed the square with magic. Took no wounds, so he did not sink. Also heard as Calvis.
- Corys. An orc labourer with a wife and a daughter at home. Was carrying groceries (oranges, bread, wine) and a doll when Eberhard burnt him. Brother Caldus brought him back from the brink. He woke in the Grand Hall and does not know who burnt him; Krag does.
- Till (Lil Ventil). A young gnome who woke in the Grand Hall and started calling into the dark. (Full name unverified.)
- The dock worker. A muscular woman in her mid-thirties who fights like a monk. She caught Eberhard's flaming blade and knocked him down. Took no wounds and did not sink.
- The grandmother. An old woman with a bladed fan, still in a rage when the square sank. Brother Caldus disarmed her.
- Darcy. Front clerk at the Old Town bathhouse Krag uses once or twice a week. Saves the plush towels for regulars.
- Anders. Owner of a small smithy in Old Town (name heard as the Copper Coban; unverified) who enchanted Krag's weapons.
- Alyssa. A halfling brawler Krag trains at the fighting pits. Went to a party in the Estates on the evening of 2 October.
