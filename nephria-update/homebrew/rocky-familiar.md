# House rule: Rocky, Brokk's familiar

Rocky is Brokk Trenevain's familiar, summoned with Find Familiar. The DM wrote this stat block for the table; where it differs from the 2024 Find Familiar spell, the stat block wins.

## Stat block

Rocky. Tiny unaligned Construct (familiar).

## Traits

- City Knowledge. Rocky cannot become lost in Nephria by magical or nonmagical means. Rocky has advantage on ability checks made to locate creatures or objects.
- Constructed Nature. Rocky does not need air, food, drink, or sleep.
- Telepathic. Rocky can communicate telepathically with Brokk while within 100 feet of him.
- Shared Senses. Brokk can see through Rocky's eyes and hear what it hears until the start of his next turn, gaining Rocky's blindsight. (The stat block names no action cost. Find Familiar makes this a Bonus Action. Ask the DM.)

## Actions

- Spelltouch. Melee spell attack, +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. On a hit, Brokk casts a touch spell or uses his Draining Strike as if he were in Rocky's space.

## Bonus actions

- Need Directions. Rocky takes the Help action.

## How Rocky differs from a standard 2024 familiar

- Rocky is a Construct. The spell offers Celestial, Fey, or Fiend.
- The spell says a familiar cannot attack. Rocky can make Spelltouch attacks, which deliver Brokk's touch spells and Draining Strike. Ask the DM whether this is its only attack.
- The spell delivers touch spells by spending the familiar's Reaction. Spelltouch is an action with an attack roll.

## Unchanged from Find Familiar (2024)

- Rocky rolls its own initiative and acts on its own turn. It is an ally to Brokk and his allies and obeys Brokk's commands.
- At 0 hit points Rocky disappears, leaving behind anything it wore or carried. Casting Find Familiar again (one hour, 10 gp of incense, can be a ritual) brings it back.
- As a Magic action Brokk can dismiss Rocky to a pocket dimension, and as a Magic action recall it to an unoccupied space within 30 feet. Brokk can also dismiss it for ever.
- Brokk can have only one familiar. Recasting the spell lets Rocky take a new eligible form.
