# House rules seen on 2 October 2026

Rulings the DM made or explained during the session. They add to `house-rules.md`; where the two disagree, ask the DM.

## Ruleset

The table plays the 2024 rules: weapon masteries (Topple), the Study and Magic actions, 2024 spell text. The table's own skills (Influence, Guile, Brawn, Tradecraft, Sanity and its sub-skills, and others) replace the standard list.

## Injuries

- When an attack against a creature is a critical hit, the target chooses: take double damage as normal, or take normal damage plus an injury.
- An injury starts with a Constitution saving throw. The DM then reads a result from an injury table for the damage type. Example: for slashing or piercing, a result of 17 to 20 is an open wound, and the creature loses a hit die's worth of hit points each turn for 5 turns. Lower results are worse.
- A natural 1 on a saving throw can also cause an injury when the DM thinks it fits. Not every time.
- The DM's intent: injuries come from the world, not as punishment for a bad roll.
- Example from this session: Krag's natural 1 gave him hallucinations of his dead companions and disadvantage on concentration checks until healed.

## Nonlethal damage

Declared before the attack. Melee attacks only, not ranged attacks or spells. Unarmed strikes can still kill unless the attacker says otherwise.

## Krag's living shadow

On a natural 1, Krag makes a DC 15 Wisdom save. On a failure he is Incapacitated until the start of his next turn, when he rolls a d8 on the Shadow's Will table to decide what the shadow does. One result: he does not move or take a Bonus Action and makes one melee attack against a random creature within reach, or takes no action if none is in reach. The shadow may choose to use his lengthened strike (twice per long rest).

## Gilding

A gilded creature gains 9 temporary hit points when it rolls initiative (Eberhard, at his level). The gilded have come back from death and do not fully remember their earlier lives.

## Hit points on level up

Rolled, rerolling 1s.

## Inspiration tokens

The DM hands out a token at the start of a session. Spending it lets a player roll a check with advantage.

## Darkvision with light

Darkvision shows darkness as dim light in grey. A creature with darkvision standing in bright light still sees grey shapes in the dark beyond the light, out to its darkvision range.

## Initiative

The DM treats a round as six seconds in which everyone acts at once; initiative is only who moves a fraction faster. Events in two places can run slightly out of step.
