## Resources

- Spell slots: five 1st-level slots, regained on a long rest. (check)
- Pact slots: three 2nd-level slots, regained on a short rest. (check)
- Out of Phase: 3 per long rest. For 1 minute, attacks against Brokk have disadvantage and he can move through other creatures' spaces.
- Inspiration: 4 per long rest. Spend one to inspire a creature within 60 ft. that can see or hear him; within the next hour it may add 1d6 to a failed roll.
- Distraction: 3 per long rest. Brokk draws focus; until the end of his next turn, attacks against creatures within 10 ft. of him that saw the display have advantage.

## Attacks

- Grapple: DC 9, Strength or Dexterity. The target is Grappled. (check DC)
- Shove: DC 9, Strength or Dexterity. Push 5 ft. or knock Prone. (check DC)
- Thunderclap: Constitution save against DC 15, 1d6 thunder to each creature within 5 ft. other than Brokk. Loud. (Sheet says 1d6; 2024 cantrips reach 2d6 at character level 5. Ask the DM.)
- Poison Spray: range 30 ft., +6 to hit, 1d12 poison, and pushes the target up to 10 ft. away. (Sheet says 1d12; see above.)
- Draining Strike: usable after taking the Magic action. +6 to hit, 1d6 + 4 plus 1d10 necrotic. Ignores resistance. Brokk gains temporary hit points equal to the damage, the target's hit point maximum drops by the same, and the target cannot heal until the end of Brokk's next turn.
- Drain Life: when Draining Strike hits, Brokk can spend a pact slot for an extra 3d8 necrotic and heals for the damage dealt.

Rocky can deliver Draining Strike and touch spells through its Spelltouch action (see `rocky-familiar.md`).

## Spells (2024 rules unless noted)

Summaries in plain terms. The cards are the authority.

Cantrips
- Chill Touch: melee spell attack, necrotic damage; the target cannot regain hit points until the end of Brokk's next turn.
- Dancing Lights: up to four torch-sized lights within 120 ft. (or one humanlike glow), each giving dim light in a 10 ft. radius; move them 60 ft. as a Bonus Action; each must stay within 20 ft. of another. Concentration, 1 minute.
- Fade (table spell): Bonus Action. Brokk is invisible until the start of his next turn, with what he carries. Ends if he attacks or casts a spell.
- Friends: one creature within 10 ft. makes a Wisdom save or is Charmed for up to 1 minute. Auto-succeeds if it is not a Humanoid, if Brokk is fighting it, or if he cast this on it in the last day. Ends if the target takes damage or Brokk attacks, deals damage, or forces a save. The target knows afterwards.
- Minor Illusion: a sound or a small image (no bigger than a 5 ft. cube) within 30 ft. for 1 minute. A creature using the Study action can see through it with Intelligence (Investigation) against DC 15.
- Poison Spray: see Attacks.
- Prestidigitation: small harmless tricks within 10 ft. (sparks, cleaning, warming, a mark, a trinket).
- Siphon (table spell): range 30 ft., up to 1 minute. Curses a creature (Charisma save if unwilling). When it would regain hit points, Brokk can use his Reaction so it regains only half and he regains the other half.
- Thunderclap: see Attacks.
- Transpose (table spell): Brokk and a willing creature he can see within 15 ft. swap places by teleport. Range grows by 5 ft. at character levels 5, 11, and 17.
- True Strike: one weapon attack using Charisma for attack and damage; damage can be radiant. Extra radiant damage at higher levels.

1st level
- Armor of Agathys: Bonus Action, 1 hour. Temporary hit points, and melee attackers who hit Brokk take cold damage. Ends when the temporary hit points run out. More of both at higher slots.
- Bane: up to three creatures within 30 ft. make a Charisma save or subtract 1d4 from attacks and saves. Concentration, 1 minute.
- Charm Person: one Humanoid within 30 ft. makes a Wisdom save (advantage if Brokk's side is fighting it) or is Charmed for 1 hour, ending early if Brokk or his allies damage it. It knows afterwards. Also castable through the Eyes of Charming.
- Command: one-word command (Approach, Drop, Flee, Grovel, Halt) to a creature within 60 ft.; Wisdom save.
- Comprehend Languages: 1 hour, ritual. Understand any spoken or signed language, and written language while touching it.
- Detect Magic: concentration up to 10 minutes, ritual. Sense magic within 30 ft. and use the Magic action to see auras and schools.
- Disguise Self: 1 hour. Change appearance, up to a foot taller or shorter. A creature using the Study action can see through it with Intelligence (Investigation) against DC 15.
- False Life: temporary hit points.
- Feather Fall: Reaction. Up to five falling creatures within 60 ft. descend slowly and land unharmed.
- Find Familiar: one hour, ritual. How Rocky is summoned or recalled.
- Fog Cloud: a 20 ft. radius sphere of heavy fog within 120 ft. Concentration, up to 1 hour.
- Identify: one minute, ritual. Learn a touched magic item's properties, or which spells affect a touched creature.
- Speak with Animals: 10 minutes, ritual. Talk with Beasts.

2nd level
- Augury: one minute, ritual. An omen (weal, woe, both, or indifference) about a course of action in the next 30 minutes. Repeat castings before a long rest risk no answer.
- Crown of Madness: a Humanoid within 120 ft. makes a Wisdom save or must attack a creature Brokk chooses each turn. Brokk must use the Magic action each turn to keep it going.
- Mirror Image: three illusory duplicates absorb hits for 1 minute. Useless against Blindsight, Truesight, or a Blinded attacker.

## Magic items

- Eyes of Charming. Wondrous item, uncommon, requires attunement, 800 crowns. 6 charges, regained at dawn. Spend 1 or more charges to cast Charm Person at DC 16, at a level equal to the charges spent.
- Obsidian Mirror. Wondrous item, very rare, requires attunement, 40,000 crowns. While holding it, cast Scrying at DC 17. Small chance that a Shadow Demon begins stalking the user. The target of a Sanctuary the user casts gains Blindsight 30 ft. (Ownership: confirm.)
- Ancient Vacoran Amulet. Wondrous item, very rare, 40,000 crowns. Tomb jade and bone. Attunes to its wearer automatically. Once per dawn while worn, cast Globe of Invulnerability. Brokk has it in his pocket and has not put it on. In the fiction he does not yet know what it does.
- Inkdrinker card. Given at the end of the 2 October session. Meaning unknown.

## Spells granted by items

- Scrying (Obsidian Mirror): 10 minutes, concentration up to 10 minutes. See and hear a creature on the same plane, which makes a Wisdom save modified by how well Brokk knows it and what he holds of it. Or fix the sensor on a place he has seen.
- Globe of Invulnerability (Vacoran Amulet): 6th level, concentration up to 1 minute. A 10 ft. barrier round the caster; spells of 5th level or lower from outside have no effect inside.
